NVC Racing Game

A browser-based 2D campus racing game built for the Wildcats AI & Cloud Technology Club at Northwest Vista College. Race across a pixel-art NVC campus map, hit all five checkpoints, and avoid getting caught by Professor Burch!

What Is It?

NVC Racing Game is a top-down arcade-style game set on a 3000×3000px pixel-art version of the NVC campus. The player navigates through five real campus landmarks while being chased by an animated professor sprite. Beat the clock — or get meowed at.

How to Play
Controls
Key	Action
W or ↑	Move up
S or ↓	Move down
A or ←	Move left
D or →	Move right
R	Restart after win or getting caught
Objective
Wait for the countdown to finish
Navigate to each numbered checkpoint in order:
1 — Start (Pecan Hall)
2 — Juniper Hall
3 — Cedar Elm STEM Center
4 — Huisache Hall
5 — Finish (Pecan Hall)
Reach all 5 checkpoints as fast as possible
Don't get caught by Professor Burch!
Tips
The minimap in the bottom-right corner shows your position, the professor's position, and all checkpoint locations
The next checkpoint pulses pink and shows its name above it when you're nearby
If you get too far away, the professor teleports closer — watch for the red screen flash warning
Your time is shown in the top-left corner and stops when you finish or get caught
